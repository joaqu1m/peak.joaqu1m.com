Mandrake

Mandrake · 5.0 kg

What it does

A mandrake that screams every 3 seconds once uprooted, making nearby scouts drowsy. Eaten raw it restores 10% hunger and hides your stamina bar for 60 seconds; only cooking it silences the scream and removes 25% curse.

Effects

  • Screams every 3 s after being picked, inflicting drowsiness on those nearby
  • Raw: restores 10% hunger
  • Raw: hides the stamina bar for 60 s
  • Cooked: stops screaming and removes 25% curse
  • Has a 50% chance of spawning in place of a Medicinal Root
