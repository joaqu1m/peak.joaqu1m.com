Blowgun

Blowgun · 5.0 kg

What it does

Single-shot blowgun: the dart clears every status effect except curse from the scout it hits, but it also inflicts 120 drowsy, knocking them out. The dart passes through you, so it only works on someone else; cooking swaps the drowsiness for 150 heat.

Effects

  • Fires a dart that clears all the target's status effects, except curse
  • Inflicts 120 drowsiness on the target
  • Does not work on yourself: the dart passes through whoever fires it
  • Single use: the blowgun disappears after firing
  • Does not appear in solo mode
  • Cooked, swaps 120 drowsiness for 150 heat
