Remedy Fungus

Remedy Fungus · 1.0 kg

What it does

Remedy fungus: eating it heals 45% injury and removes 30% poison and 30% spores. Dropped, it bursts into an 8 m healing cloud that removes 17.5 injury at once and then up to 27.5 injury, poison and spores each, at 2.5 per second over 11 seconds, for anyone standing inside it.

Effects

  • Eaten: heals 45% injury and removes 30% poison and 30% spores
  • Dropped: creates a healing cloud with a range of 8 m
  • The cloud removes 17.5 injury immediately
  • Inside the cloud, removes up to 27.5 injury, poison, and spores (2.5/s for 11 s)
