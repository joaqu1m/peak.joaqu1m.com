Bugle of Friendship

Bugle of Friendship · 5.0 kg

What it does

Bugle of Friendship: playing it grants infinite stamina to every scout in a large radius, yourself included, but only for a total of 7.5 seconds of playing time. It does not spawn in solo play.

Effects

  • Plays a musical note when used
  • Gives infinite stamina to all scouts within a wide radius
  • The effect also reaches whoever is touching it
  • Total limit of 7.5 seconds of touch
  • Does not appear in solo mode
