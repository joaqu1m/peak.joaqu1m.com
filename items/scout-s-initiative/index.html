Scout's Initiative

Scout's Initiative · 5.0 kg

What it does

Amulet that launches you and nearby players into a super jump, with low gravity right after the leap. Every use builds up petrification, and the amulet stops working once your petrification is maxed out.

Effects

  • Super jump on you and on players within range
  • Reduced gravity for a few moments after jumping
  • Accumulates petrify with each use
  • Using it activates the amulet instead of consuming it — it never runs out
  • Becomes unusable when petrify is at maximum

Where to find it
