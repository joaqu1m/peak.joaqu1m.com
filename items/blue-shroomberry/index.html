Blue Shroomberry

Blue Shroomberry · 5.0 kg

What it does

Food that restores 10% hunger and inflicts 5 spores. Eating it always triggers a bad mushroom effect — a spore burst, blindness and the like — so it is a berry to avoid; cooking removes the spores.

Effects

  • Restores 10% hunger
  • Inflicts 5 spores
  • Always triggers a bad mushroom effect (spore explosion, blindness, etc.)
  • Cooking removes the spores effect
  • Can be used on a friend
