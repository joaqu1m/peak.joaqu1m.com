Faerie Lantern

Faerie Lantern · 5.0 kg

What it does

Faerie lantern with 30 fuel: while lit it holds a radius-7 status protection zone around itself that steadily heals nearby scouts, removing up to 75 heat, 150 cold, 75 poison, 75 spores, 150 drowsiness and 75 injury over 30 seconds. It can be snuffed out to save the remaining fuel, stays lit inside a dropped Backpack, and explodes if cooked, curing everyone within 4.8 m.

Effects

  • Lights/extinguishes when used
  • Lit, creates a status protection zone with radius 7
  • Heals those nearby: up to 75 heat, 150 cold, 75 poison, 75 spores, 150 drowsiness, and 75 injury over 30 s
  • Initial fuel: 30
  • Can be put out to save the remaining fuel
  • Stays lit when stored in a dropped backpack
  • Cooked, explodes and removes up to 25 poison, cold, heat, drowsiness, and thorns within a radius of 4.8 m
