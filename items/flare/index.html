Flare

Flare · 5.0 kg

What it does

Single-use flare: light it for a tall column of coloured smoke in your scout's colour to mark your position — and if you light it at the PEAK, it summons the rescue helicopter. Cooking it makes it explode, dealing up to 20% injury to everyone within 4.8 m.
