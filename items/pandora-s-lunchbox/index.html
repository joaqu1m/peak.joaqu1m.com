Pandora's Lunchbox

Pandora's Lunchbox · 5.0 kg

What it does

Pandora's lunchbox, 3 uses: every opening clears all of your statuses, curse included, then scatters a random amount of bad status across cold, heat, poison, drowsiness, injury, hunger, spores and curse. It also rolls a helping of bonus stamina.

Effects

  • Clears all status effects, including curse
  • Applies a random amount of negative status effects, drawn from cold, heat, poison, drowsiness, injury, hunger, spores, and curse
  • Also rolls a random amount of bonus stamina
  • 3 uses; disappears when the last one is used
