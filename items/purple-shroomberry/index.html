Purple Shroomberry

Purple Shroomberry · 5.0 kg

What it does

Food that restores 10% hunger and increases spores by 5%. It also has a random mushroom effect: it can be great or terrible.

Effects

  • Random mushroom effect: can be good (infinite stamina, speed, low gravity, invincibility, healing) or bad (spore explosion, blindness, etc.)
  • Restores 10% hunger
  • Increases spores by 5%
  • Cooking neutralizes its special effects
