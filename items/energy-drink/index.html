Energy Drink

Energy Drink · 5.0 kg

What it does

Energy drink: while it lasts you move and climb faster and drowsiness cannot build up. It wipes your sleepiness and removes 30% heat immediately, but when it wears off tiredness hits back. You can give it to a friend too.

Effects

  • Increases movement and climbing speed for a while
  • Zeroes out drowsiness when drunk
  • Reduces heat by 30%
  • Holds drowsiness at zero while the effect lasts
  • Returns part of the drowsiness when the effect ends
  • Consumed when used
  • Can be used on a friend
