The Book of Bones

The Book of Bones · 5.0 kg

What it does

The Book of Bones (single use): reading it turns you into a skeleton — or back again — and clears your other statuses, but it never removes curse: using it inflicts 25% curse. Cooking the book removes that curse effect.

Effects

  • Turns you into a skeleton (and back)
  • Clears your other status effects; curse is not cleared
  • Inflicts 25% curse when used
  • Single use
  • Cooking removes the curse effect
