Cursed Skull

Cursed Skull · 5.0 kg

What it does

Cursed skull: using it instantly kills the scout holding it, and in exchange every other player nearby gains 50% bonus stamina and is cleansed of all negative statuses except curse. It is only found in Ancient Luggage and never spawns in solo play.

Effects

  • Instantly kills whoever uses it
  • Gives 50% bonus stamina to other players within range
  • Clears their negative status effects, except curse
  • Only found in Ancient Luggage
  • Does not appear in solo mode
  • Cooked, activates instantly and then explodes like dynamite
