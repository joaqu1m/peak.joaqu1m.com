Checkpoint Flag

Checkpoint Flag · 5.0 kg

What it does

Checkpoint flag: plant it to create a single-use respawn point for yourself only — nobody else respawns there. It saves your statuses as they were when it was planted and restores them when you respawn.

Effects

  • Creates a single-use respawn checkpoint
  • Only applies to whoever planted it, not the group
  • Saves your status effects at the moment of planting and restores them on respawn
  • Can only be cooked with explosives
