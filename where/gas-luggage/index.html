Gas (trick) luggage

The trick luggage looks exactly like a small luggage, but releases poison gas when opened. In the game files they only exist inside the Citadel tower, spread across its floors — treat them as a trap to avoid on the climb. Their positions are baked per map, but the game enables them at runtime, so a given one may not be active in every run.
